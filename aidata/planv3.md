The progress bar at the top should be a vibrant accent color (like Revolut Blue or a 'Success Green') to keep the user engaged.
viii.	Implement Micro-Interactions. When I hover over a button, it should slightly scale up (scale: 1.02). When I click 'Next', the transition should feel like a 'slide' or a 'fade-and-lift'. Use a blur effect (backdrop-filter: blur(10px)) on the navigation bar so it looks transparent when scrolling.
